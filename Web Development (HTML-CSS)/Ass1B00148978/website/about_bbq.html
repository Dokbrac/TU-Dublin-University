American barbecue as we know it was born in the south. Southerners used pigs, because they needed less space and could be let loose when feed was scarce to wander and eat. Because these wandering pigs were leaner, they needed the slow cooking method of BBQ to tenderize. Hog BBQ actually became a point of contention for patriotism, and Southerners were not willing to export their pork to the north. Just before the Civil War, Southerners ate one pound of beef for every five pounds of pork. It was around this time that BBQ and its styles branched out throughout the country.
